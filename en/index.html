Frequently asked questions

Where is the First Euroflat Hotel?

Boulevard Charlemagne 50, 1000 Brussels, in the heart of the European Quarter, 100 metres from Schuman metro station. The Grand-Place is 15 minutes away.

How many rooms does the hotel have?

133 rooms of 35 to 45 m², all with a kitchenette: Classic, Garden, Business and Junior Suite.

Does the hotel have parking?

Yes, a private garage with 10 spaces, at €15 per day.

Are pets allowed?

Yes, pets under 20 kg are welcome, from €20 per stay. Please let the hotel know before you arrive.

How do I get to the airport?

Brussels Airport is 20 minutes away by train from Schuman station, or by taxi for €38 to €40 one way. Charleroi Airport is 55 minutes away, with a shuttle from a stop near the hotel.

How many people do the meeting rooms hold?

The Grand Charlemagne room (90 m²) seats up to 80 people theatre-style, 52 classroom-style, 40 boardroom-style and 36 in a U-shape. The Charlemagne I or II rooms (45 m²) seat up to 30 people. Day packages at €75 or €85 per person.

Is the wifi free?

Yes, throughout the hotel. Reception is open 24 hours a day.
